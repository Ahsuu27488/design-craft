# Taste profile: what the owner likes

Owner: Muhammad Ahsan (web + AI automation developer; sells to small businesses abroad). Update this file when he gives new feedback; it outranks the generic defaults in this skill.

## He wants
- **2026-level premium**: the feel of Apple product pages, Linear, Vercel and Awwwards sites: confident, polished, alive.
- **Smooth everything**: smooth scroll (Lenis) on marketing pages, smooth transitions between states and pages, springy but quick UI feedback.
- **Scroll effects**: reveals, a pinned scroll story or a scroll-linked hero, parallax depth. One choreographed moment per section, not noise.
- **Tasteful 3D**: a 3D or shader hero piece where it fits (he rated his Three.js Relay landing 7/10 and wants better). Lighter, faster and more intentional than "a lot of 3D".
- **Hover and micro-interactions**: magnetic buttons, spotlight cards, tilt, press feedback, cursor-aware details on desktop.
- **Apple Liquid Glass** on the floating layer (nav pill, dock, chat composer, modals, primary CTA).
- **Depth and richness**: real imagery, layered surfaces, light, gradients of light (aurora/beam/spotlight used as ONE signature light effect), not empty pages.
- Dark premium themes work well for his tech demos; light themes must still feel rich, not bare.
- It must not look AI-generated or vibe-coded.

## He does not want
- **The flat, solid-colour, minimalist "artistic" look** that the Taste skill (`design-taste-frontend`) produces: whole fields of flat colour, no gradients at all, no ornament, editorial emptiness. He tried it and disliked it. Do not use Taste by default; only when he explicitly asks for an editorial/minimal look.
- Generic AI tells: purple-to-blue gradient everything, gradient text, neon glow borders, identical 3-card rows, cards inside cards, Inter everywhere, emoji icons, ALL-CAPS eyebrow labels on every heading, `→` on every button, fake dashboards of grey bars.
- Glass everywhere (cheap, kills contrast), five competing effects, slow preloaders, scroll-jacking.
- Designs that only work on desktop.

## Defaults when he gives no direction
- Dark base `oklch(0.14 0.01 270)` area, surfaces stepping up ~0.03 lightness, borders white 6 to 10%, text white 92/64/44%, ONE accent chosen for the product, one signature light effect.
- A deliberate type pairing (e.g. Geist / Instrument Sans / Hanken Grotesk / Satoshi for UI, a display face or Instrument Serif accent for headlines), large confident headlines, tight display tracking.
- Bento or asymmetric layouts instead of identical grids.
- Lenis + reveals + one scroll story + glass nav + one 3D/shader moment on landing pages; restrained, fast UI inside apps.

## How he works
- Replies in Roman Urdu in the agency repo; code, docs and commits in English.
- He wants to see before/after screenshots and short, honest reports. Ask before big direction changes; show samples when a choice is about taste.
