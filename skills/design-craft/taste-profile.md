# Taste profile

design-craft ships with its author's taste (Muhammad Ahsan, web + AI developer). It outranks the generic defaults in this skill, and the project's own brief outranks it. **Make it yours:** fork the repo and edit this file, or tell Claude "add to my design-craft taste profile: ..." and keep your copy in git.

## Wanted
- **2026-level premium**: the feel of Apple product pages, Linear, Vercel and Awwwards sites: confident, polished, alive.
- **Smooth everything**: smooth scroll (Lenis) on marketing pages, smooth transitions between states and pages, springy but quick UI feedback.
- **Scroll effects**: reveals, a pinned scroll story or a scroll-linked hero, parallax depth. One choreographed moment per section, not noise.
- **Tasteful 3D**: a 3D or shader hero piece where it fits. Lighter, faster and more intentional than "a lot of 3D".
- **Hover and micro-interactions**: magnetic buttons, spotlight cards, tilt, press feedback, cursor-aware details on desktop.
- **Apple Liquid Glass** on the floating layer (nav pill, dock, chat composer, modals, primary CTA).
- **Depth and richness**: real imagery, layered surfaces, light (aurora / beam / spotlight used as ONE signature light effect), not empty pages.
- Dark premium themes for tech products; light themes must still feel rich, not bare.
- It must never look AI-generated or vibe-coded.

## Not wanted
- **The flat, solid-colour, minimalist "artistic" look**: whole fields of flat colour, no gradients at all, no ornament, editorial emptiness. This is what the Taste skill (`design-taste-frontend`) produces, so Taste is off by default and only used when this look is explicitly requested.
- Generic AI tells: purple-to-blue gradient everything, gradient text, neon glow borders, identical 3-card rows, cards inside cards, Inter everywhere, emoji icons, ALL-CAPS eyebrow labels on every heading, `→` on every button, fake dashboards of grey bars.
- Glass everywhere (cheap, kills contrast), five competing effects, slow preloaders, scroll-jacking.
- Designs that only work on desktop.

## Defaults when there is no direction
- Dark base around `oklch(0.14 0.01 270)`, surfaces stepping up ~0.03 lightness, borders white 6 to 10%, text white 92 / 64 / 44%, ONE accent chosen for the product, one signature light effect.
- A deliberate type pairing (e.g. Geist / Instrument Sans / Hanken Grotesk / Satoshi for UI, a display face or Instrument Serif accent for headlines), large confident headlines, tight display tracking.
- Bento or asymmetric layouts instead of identical grids.
- Landing pages: Lenis + reveals + one scroll story + glass nav + one 3D/shader moment. Inside apps: restrained, fast UI.

## Working style
- Show before/after screenshots and short, honest reports.
- Ask before big direction changes; show 2 or 3 samples when a choice is about taste.
